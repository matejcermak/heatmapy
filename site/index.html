Why Mapy.com

Terrain, trails and closures on one map

Strava's map is fine for looking at your activities, but it shows too little for planning. The Mapy.com outdoor map shows terrain, small paths and surfaces.

⛰️

Hill shading and contours

See where the hills, forests and open fields are before you set off.

🛤️

Even the smallest paths

From motorways to narrow forest singletrack. Each type of road or path has its own style, so you can tell them apart.

🚧

Surfaces and closures

The map shows road types, surfaces and current closures, so you can stay off busy roads and closed sections.

🧭

Route planner

Routes follow real paths, road and mountain bikes get separate modes, and ⌘/Ctrl-click adds a point.

Mapy.com vs Strava

The same place on both maps

Drag the slider to compare. Mapy.com is on the left, Strava on the right.

Terrain and road types

Hill shading and contours show whether the ground is hilly or flat, forest or open. Main roads, quiet lanes and gravel or forest tracks each have their own colour, which makes it easy to keep a route off busy roads. On Strava's map the same area looks flat and most roads look alike.

Mapy.com Strava

Small trails and route planning

Mapy.com has small trails even in remote areas, along with current closures. The planner keeps the route on real paths and has separate road and MTB modes. Strava's map leaves out many of these paths.
